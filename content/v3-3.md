# v3.3: Collaborative Workbench

```{admonition} Forthcoming release
:class: important
The v3.3 **Collaborative Workbench** — a browser-first, collaborative tool for reconciling and
authoring place data — is in development and available to WHG staff and invited beta testers only.
It is not yet released to the public. These pages document features as they are built; when v3.3
ships they will move into **Guides & Tutorials**.
```

The **Workbench** menu — one tool, many document types (Map your Data, Place
Collection, Itinerary, Gazetteer Group), with entry points to edit published
material and review suggested corrections.
