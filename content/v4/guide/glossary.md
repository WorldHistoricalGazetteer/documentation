# Glossary: Linking Records

```{note}
Part of the [Draft v4 User Guide](../userguide_index.md): under review during beta testing.
```

These are the words WHG v4 uses on screen when you say how records relate to one another. Where the
Record page or the Atlas shows one of these terms, this is what it means.

## Link

A **link** is a statement you make that two records are, or are not, the same place. It is made in the
**Link sheet**, opened from the Atlas, a Record page or a [Locus](./loci.md) page. A link is
attributed to you, dated, and can be taken back. It is never final: someone else may disagree, and both
statements are kept. See [Linking records: same place, or not](./record-pages.md#linking-records-same-place-or-not).

## Same place

You think the two records describe the same place. This is the default choice in the Link sheet.
Use it when the descriptions plausibly agree, even if their details differ (a different spelling, a
slightly different position, a different date range).

## Same place, exactly

Under **Advanced** in the Link sheet. You are sure the two records mean one and the same place. Use it
rarely; **Same place** is right in most cases.

## Not the same place

You are sure the two records describe different places. This is a deliberate statement, and it asks for
a reason. It is not the same as passing over a suggestion: ignoring a suggestion records nothing about the
world.

## None of these fits

None of the suggested records is the same place as yours. This is a note about your review of one record,
not a statement about the world. It can go out of date when new records are added, and it is shown only to you
and your dataset team.

## Not sure yet

You want to come back to this record later. Nothing is recorded about the places themselves.

## Not yet checked

WHG has not yet been able to look for suggestions for this record.

## Suggestion

A record WHG proposes as possibly the same place as one of yours. Suggestions are ordered by how likely
they are:

- **Likely the same place**: one clear suggestion.
- **Which one, if any?**: several plausible suggestions.
- **Weak suggestions**: possible, but unconvincing.
- **Nothing found**: no suggestions.
- **Later**: records you set aside with *Not sure yet*.

Choosing a suggestion makes a link. Passing it over makes nothing.

## Finding suggestions

The automatic search that proposes suggestions. It never makes a link on your behalf.

## Links (section of a Record page)

The Record page section that lists what has been stated about which other records describe the same
place. It is currently headed **Concordance** and is being renamed **Links**. It shows who made each
link and when. See [Record Pages and the Concordance](./record-pages.md).

## Record

One contributor's account of a place, with its own names, locations, dates and sources. Several records
may describe the same place.

## Locus

A page that groups records that may be one place, so that the group can be cited. A Locus is not a record,
and it is not itself a statement that the records are the same place. See [Loci](./loci.md).

## Private until your dataset is published

A link that involves a record in a dataset that is not yet public is visible only to you, your dataset
team and WHG staff. It becomes visible when the dataset is published.

## Review by the WHG team

Every dataset is reviewed by the WHG team before publication during beta. The review is separate from
linking. On a dataset in the review process you answer the two questions on Indigenous and sensitive data,
then choose **Submit for review**. An editor opens the review queue and chooses **Start review**, then
**Accept**, **Request changes** (with a note) or **Decline** (with a note). After changes are requested, or a
decline, you can edit the dataset and use **Submit again for review**; a withdrawn dataset is also reviewed
again before it can be published. A dataset that is already public must be made private before you can
choose **Start the review process**. The steps, the labels and the emails are described in
[Review before publication](./linking-your-records.md#review-before-publication).

## Older WHG words

**Reconciliation** is the older WHG term for what v4 calls linking your records. The earlier
reconciliation tools remain available during beta testing, alongside linking; see
[Guides & Tutorials](../../guides.md).
