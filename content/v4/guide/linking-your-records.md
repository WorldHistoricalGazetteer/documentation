A **Link** is your statement that one of your records and another record describe the same place, or that
they do not. Linking is optional: your dataset is published and searchable whether or not you link
anything. Links are an enrichment that helps others find your records beside related ones.

## Starting

Open your dataset's status page. Its **Links** card (marked *beta*) shows how much you have reviewed and
how many Links you have made. Choose **Link your records** (or **Continue linking** once you have started).

If no suggestions have been prepared yet, choose **Prepare suggestions**. WHG then looks for records that
may be the same places as yours. This takes a while; you can start as soon as some are ready, and more will
appear. Suggestions are drawn from WHG's public records. You can begin while your dataset is still private,
and nothing of yours becomes visible by doing so.

## The suggestions feed

The page lists your records in tabs, ordered by how likely a match is:

| Tab | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Likely the same place** | The suggestion and your record agree on the name and at least one other thing. Check before you confirm: a perfect name can still be a lake or a station named after the place. |
| **Which one, if any?** | More than one place fits, or only the name agrees. Choose one, or say none fits. |
| **Weak suggestions** | Optional. Looking is up to you. |
| **Nothing found** | Normal. Search by hand if you know a spelling, or leave it. |
| **Later** | Records you set aside. They stay until you decide. |
| **Not yet checked** | The search service did not answer for these records yet. They will be tried again; this is not the same as nothing found. |

The list on the left shows the records in the tab; the right shows one record at a time with its
suggestions. Each suggestion gives its source, type, distance from your record, dates, country and a short
sentence saying why it was suggested. A suggestion that looks like a building, lake or station named after a
place, rather than the place itself, carries a warning.

If you know a better match, use the search box to look for another name.

## Making a decision

For the record on screen you can:

- **Same place**: choose one or more suggestions (keys 1 to 9) and confirm. Each becomes a Link.
- **Not the same place**, beside an individual suggestion: you are passing that suggestion over. This
  records nothing about the world. In the **Which one, if any?** tab, **Advanced** offers a box to also record the
  answer as a Link that others can see.
- **None of these fits**: none of the suggestions is right. This is a note about your review, not a
  statement about the world, and it can go out of date when new records are added.
- **Not sure yet**: set the record aside in **Later**.
- **Undo** takes back your last decision.

You may add a note (up to 2000 characters) saying why you decided. Keyboard shortcuts are listed under the
feed: Enter for Same place, `x` for Not the same place, `n` for None of these fits, `l` for Not sure yet,
`u` for Undo, `s` to search, and the arrow keys to move between records.

### Same place, exactly

Under **Advanced** you can choose **Same place, exactly** instead of **Same place**. Use it only when the two
records are interchangeable in every respect: the same place, the same extent, the same period. **Same place**
is right in most cases and is the default.

## The Link sheet

Elsewhere in WHG, the same choices appear in the **Link sheet** (titled *Link two places*). It opens from
the **Link** button in the Atlas, from the Same place and Not the same buttons on a
[Record page](./record-pages.md) and from the member rows of a [Locus](./loci.md) page. It offers
**Same place**, **Not the same place** and, under **Advanced**, **Same place, exactly**, with an optional
*Why?* box. Choose **Link** to save. You can open the sheet again to change or withdraw a Link you made.

## What a Link does

A Link is a statement by a person, not a decision by WHG. It does not merge records or alter anyone's
data. Once a Link is public:

- it appears in the **Concordance** of the Record pages of both records, labelled *Linked by* your name and
  date. A Link made by someone on a record's dataset team counts as that dataset's own statement; a Link by
  anyone else counts as another person's. See [Record Pages and the Concordance](./record-pages.md);
- it appears in the **Links made by people** section of the Record page, where any signed-in user can
  **dispute** it with a Link of their own. A dispute does not delete your Link: both stay visible;
- **Not the same place** Links are listed separately and never join records together.

Links are published under CC BY 4.0. You may make up to 200 new Links a day.

## Private until your dataset is published

If either record belongs to a dataset that is not yet public, your Link is saved as **pending**: only you
and that dataset's owners can see it, and nothing is shared outside WHG. It becomes visible when the dataset
is published.

## Review by the WHG team

During beta, every dataset is reviewed by the WHG team before it is published. Review is separate from
linking: you do not need to finish, or start, linking for your dataset to be reviewed.

## Older v3 tools

The earlier WHG matching tools (called *reconciliation* in v3) still work during beta. A dataset can be
linked with both. See [Guides & Tutorials](../../guides.md) for how they work.
